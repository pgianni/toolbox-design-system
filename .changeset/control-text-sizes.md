Harmonize text sizes on the control scale: sm and md controls (buttons, fields, Select, Textarea, NumberInput, SegmentedControl) are all 16 px on mobile and 14 px from `md` up; lg controls are 16 px everywhere. `controlTextClassName` exposes this scale. `Tabs` gets an optional `size` (sm, md, lg) so pill tabs, with or without container, line up with form controls.
